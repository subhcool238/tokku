Connect Notion

Tokku reads your task list so he can name your most urgent task. He can only read. He never changes anything in Notion.

  1. Open notion.so/my-integrations in your browser and create a new integration. Give it Read content only.
  2. In Notion, open your tasks database, click ... then Connections, and add that integration.
  3. Paste the integration token and the database link below.

The token is stored encrypted on this PC with Windows data protection. It is never written to the project folder.

Partner PIN

Hand this screen to your partner. They choose a PIN of 4 to 6 digits and keep it to themselves. Tokku will ask for it before he can be turned off.

Partner's phone (optional)

Lets the user send one message: "Sanky wants to turn Tokku off. Share the PIN only if you agree." Nothing else is ever sent.

On the partner's phone: install the free app ntfy, tap +, and subscribe to this topic. Keep it private.

Only a scrambled fingerprint of the PIN is stored, never the PIN itself. Your partner can only unlock. They never see what you were doing.

Turn Tokku off

Ask your partner for the PIN.

Emergency exit

Always available, no PIN needed. Hold the button for 30 seconds, then say why. The reason is saved on this PC.
