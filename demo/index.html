Tokku

A small mongoose who lives in the corner of your desktop and guards your focus. Try him below.

Browser preview. The real app runs on Windows and reacts to the apps you actually open, your Notion tasks and your phone. Here those are simulated with buttons, and every timer is a few seconds long.

Observes, never acts

He suggests. He never closes, blocks or minimises anything.

Private by design

Window titles are classified and thrown away. Nothing about what you do leaves the PC.

Quiet on calls

Mic or camera in use, a meeting or a presentation: he turns around and says nothing.
